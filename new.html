New year, new menu

With the new year in full swing, our High Life Café is serving up a nutritious spread.

Choose from warming veg-packed bowls from Pollen + Grace alongside health shots, healthy locally made snacks, and herbal teas for the perfect seasonal detox.

Enjoy Grind Coffee on our short-haul flights

We're pleased to announce our new partnership with London-based brand Grind Coffee.

When you fly Euro Traveller routes with us from London Gatwick or London Heathrow, you can pre-order or buy Grind Coffee during your flight.
