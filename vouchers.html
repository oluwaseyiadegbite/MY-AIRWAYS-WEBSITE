Using Your Voucher

Find out how to book a flight or holiday using a voucher. Vouchers are valid until 30 September 2025.

How to Redeem Your Voucher

  1. Check Your Voucher Details: Ensure your voucher is valid and check the expiration date.
  2. Go to the Booking Page: Select your flight or holiday package.
  3. Enter Your Voucher Code: Apply the code at checkout.
  4. Confirm Your Booking: Finalize payment and receive your confirmation email.

Terms & Conditions

  • Vouchers are non-transferable and can only be used by the person named on the voucher.
  • Vouchers cannot be exchanged for cash.
  • Only valid for flights and holidays booked through Seyi Airways.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use multiple vouchers for one booking?
No, only one voucher per booking is allowed.

What happens if I cancel my flight after using a voucher?
The voucher will not be refunded.

Can I use my voucher for someone else’s booking?
No, the voucher must match the passenger’s name.
